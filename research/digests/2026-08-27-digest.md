## Highest-value corrections to our guidance

1. **LTX distilled negative prompts are INERT BY CONSTRUCTION.** [OFFICIAL + issue #919] CFG=1 makes ComfyUI's CFGGuider skip the uncond pass entirely — yet official LTX-2.5 templates *ship a decorative default negative*. Community workaround is NAG, not CFG. → Validator/KB must say: on the default distilled path, LTX negatives do nothing; write exclusions into the positive.
2. **LTX-2.5's prompt enhancer was ON by default until 08-20** [TESTED + OFFICIAL fix PR #1166], silently rewriting fully-specified prompts ("rewrote a studio presenter into a bloodied stranger in a forest"). All community LTX-2.5 verdicts dated 08-11→08-20 are confounded. Verification protocol worth teaching: **ComfyUI embeds the executed graph in the output mp4 — read the metadata to see what text the encoder actually received.**
3. **Wan 2.2's weakness needs SPLITTING.** Camera-following weakness stands (ecosystem-shaped: a 250K-download checkpoint family exists to patch it) — but two August benchmarks put Wan 2.2 at the **top** for spatiotemporal consistency (Sci-VBench 2.79, highest of ANY model incl. Sora-2/Veo-3.1; SemComp best-open continuity). Its real weakness is semantic/reasoning fidelity. Vocabulary fixes: official token is **"arc shot" not "orbit"** (orbit is the word that failed); **one camera verb per shot, placed first**; **avoid the word "cinematic" on Wan** (triggers stylization branch); text cannot lock a start framing against model defaults (use T2I→I2V). And do NOT imply LTX out-follows Wan — its ecosystem shape (camera IC-LoRAs "because prompting doesn't work", seed-hunter tools) says both are bad; no measurement exists.
4. **Prompt-expansion doctrine needs refining, not discarding.** [TESTED both ways] Sci-VBench: *constrained* rewrites of *deliberately minimal* prompts gained on every dimension (+23–52% SCC). New Qwen-Image replication (2606.08492): zero-shot LLM rewriting scores below raw on all judges; "blind textual expansion pollutes the original intent." Reconciled position: **expansion helps when the prompt is underspecified AND the rewriter can't invent; hurts when the prompt is specified and the rewriter is free.** (Our app's preserve-user-words schema-constrained design is exactly the safe half — keep it, cite both results.)
5. **Distillation + LoRA = partial de-distillation.** [OFFICIAL ×2 + TESTED] Krea officially: train on RAW, infer on Turbo; 60h community A/B prefers RAW+turbo-LoRA@0.6; Z-Image LoRAs need 20 steps/CFG 1.5–2.0 (not 8/CFG 0); alibaba-pai re-distills after control training. Teachable rule: **stacking a LoRA/control on a Turbo checkpoint requires raising steps and CFG above the official recipe.**
6. **"Long prompts collapse seed diversity" is NOT general — scope it.** Counter-evidence: SemComp (brief prompts beat detailed on measured consistency axes — weak n, but *quality* not diversity), a variance-tool author claiming the opposite mechanism, Krea 2 users getting *no* seed variation at any length, Z-Image Base giving "amazing" variation. Lead with token-budget **dilution**; keep the diversity claim scoped to models where it was measured (PromptMoG's).
7. **No portable "quality tag" concept across SDXL-family.** Five incompatible official schemes (Pony V6's own card *forbids* the tags guides recommend; NoobAI has a defined time-decayed ladder; Animagine 4.0 uses its own vocabulary *at the END*; Illustrious de-biased). Sampler advice equally non-portable (NoobAI bans Karras; RealVis requires it; Pony Realism bans DPM++ 2M Karras). Our sdxlAnime target's single default is wrong for ≥3 of 5 — needs a checkpoint note.
8. **New adherence variables we don't teach:** resolution (4 model families: adherence improves near training canvas — H3 <1MP "very noisy", LTX camera-following improves with res); speed accelerators (sparse attention/TeaCache) **spend prompt adherence**, early steps own adherence (two-source: community + VGI-Bench self-correction <1%); accelerator nodes can silently disable negative-prompt nodes (same patch point).
9. **SCAIL-2 / Wan-Animate class is prompt-INERT** ("prompts have limited impact" [LORE] + no paper ablation) — classify as driving-video-conditioned; levers are masks (trained-in exact color palette! deviating degrades multi-identity), canvas 512×896, overlap arithmetic. Note their *opposite* dialects (SCAIL: describe final video incl. action, English; Animate-2: Chinese two-field, appearance-only). Also: zai-org/SCAIL-2 has **no main branch** (default `wan-scail2`) — corpus URLs keyed to @main 404.
10. **Qwen suffix retirement:** confirmed at code level (no `positive_magic` in 2512), but unresolved in community practice (Civitai staff showcases still use "4k"); exact legacy string is `", Ultra HD, 4K, cinematic composition."` (leading comma-space). Keep our position; adjust attribution.
