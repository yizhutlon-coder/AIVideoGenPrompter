## Tested findings

**Nothing in this window clears a `[TESTED]` bar.** No new controlled experiment, benchmark or
methodology-stating probe was found on any in-scope surface. The two items below are deterministic
static observations, offered at their honest grade.

1. **The validator's `_cfg_pp` sampler check has a naming hole, found by reading the v0.35.0 changelog.**
   `[OFFICIAL]` + `[SYNTHESIS]`. ComfyUI v0.35.0 (2026-09-09) adds a sampler named **`cfgpp_ud10_ab`**
   (PR #15951, *"a CFG++ sampler that works well at lower steps on Anima"*). The corpus's Krea 2 rule
   (`krea-character-art.md` §*Validator changes*) says to *"warn specifically if the sampler name
   contains `cfg_pp` and `ConditioningZeroOut` is present."* **`cfgpp_ud10_ab` does not contain the
   substring `cfg_pp`.** Any implementation of that rule as written silently skips the newest CFG++
   sampler in core. The fix is a case-insensitive match on `cfg` followed by `pp` with an optional
   separator. ⚠ Whether `cfgpp_ud10_ab` actually computes an uncond at CFG 1 was **not** verified — the
   naming gap is the finding, not the behaviour.

2. **The Krea 2 harvest is a static analysis of an official corpus, and it produced one falsification.**
   `[TESTED — static analysis of an OFFICIAL corpus]` Full working in
   [`_addenda/krea-character-art.md`](../_addenda/krea-character-art.md) §*2026-09-10 official
   prompt-pair harvest*. Headline: across the vendor's own 36 published prompt+image pairs, the
   descriptor-slot **fill rates** are colour 94% / spatial 94% / size 92% / texture 61% / shape 56% /
   quantity 53% / **text 6%**, and the **prompts do not follow the encoder descriptor's declared
   order**. The 2026-09-03 claim *"nine named slots **in that order** … a better-evidenced ordering rule
   than the app's medium-first heuristic"* survives as a *checklist* and **fails as an ordering rule**,
   against the same vendor's own prompts. See *Contradicts our current guidance* #1–#4.

## Contradicts our current guidance

Numbered as in previous digests. Items 1–6 all come from the Krea 2 harvest and all rest on the same
`[OFFICIAL]` corpus — the vendor's own 36 published prompt+image pairs — so they are unusually cheap to
verify and unusually hard to argue with. Full working in
[`_addenda/krea-character-art.md`](../_addenda/krea-character-art.md) §*2026-09-10 official prompt-pair
harvest* → *Comparison with the app's `krea2` system prompt*.

1. **`TARGETS.krea2.system`'s `ONE prose paragraph, 80-140 words` is 42%-compliant with the vendor's own
   prompts.** `[OFFICIAL]` Across the 36: **10–230 words**, median **101.5**, IQR **71–142**; 12 fall
   below 80 and 9 above 140. Krea publishes a **ten-word** prompt (`immense rocket launch exhaust as
   seen from extremely close up`) and a **230-word** prompt in the same gallery. The band is a defensible
   *craft* recommendation from `[LORE]` (§2.3) — it is not a vendor spec, and the app states it as a hard
   rule. Fix: state it as a typical range, and make the *hard* number the 512-position cap.

2. **`LEAD WITH THE MEDIUM` (in caps, as a rule) is true of two thirds of the vendor's prompts and false
   of the rest.** `[OFFICIAL]` Medium named in the first 80 characters in **24/36**; named only past the
   midpoint in **8** (relative positions up to 0.84); and **never named at all in 4**. Worse for the
   rule, the vendor's most common long-form shape discloses the medium *late and hedged*
   (`The medium appears to be a painting, rendered with visible brushstrokes and a soft focus.`) and
   pushes style into a **trailing tail**. Fix: task-scope it — lead with the medium when the medium *is*
   the point (§2.8's reading is still right for that case), otherwise lead with the subject.

3. **The 2026-09-03 claim that the encoder's nine descriptor slots are an ordering rule is falsified.**
   `[OFFICIAL]` vs `[SYNTHESIS]`. The descriptor's order is colour → shape → size → texture → quantity →
   text → spatial → objects → background. The vendor's own prompts run **objects → attributes → spatial
   → background → composition → medium/camera → lighting → colour palette**, with lighting and palette
   at mean relative positions 0.79 and 0.76. And the slots are not equally used: **colour 94% / spatial
   94% / size 92% / texture 61% / shape 56% / quantity 53% / text 6%.** Keep the nine slots as an **axis
   checklist**; delete "in that order". This also un-does the 09-03 digest's framing of it as *"a
   better-evidenced ordering rule than the app's medium-first heuristic"* — it is better-evidenced as a
   *checklist*, and it is not an ordering rule at all.

4. **The app's anti-gloss vocabulary is not vendor language.** `[OFFICIAL]` scoped absence.
   `matte surface`, `no specular highlights`, `dry pigment finish` and `flat graphic design` appear
   **nowhere** in the 36; `matte` occurs exactly once, as an object's paint colour
   (`3D rendered matte black designer toy figure`). Krea's own anti-gloss route is **naming the
   mark-making**: `expressive thick brushstrokes`, `blocky painterly brushstrokes`, `flat shading`,
   `granular stippled shading`, `volumetric grain`, `grainy paper texture`, `clean ligne claire drawing
   aesthetic`, `meticulous stippling and cross-hatching`. The phrases are still *good advice* — regrade
   them `[SYNTHESIS]` rather than presenting them as the model's own vocabulary.

5. **`Output ONLY the paragraph.` forbids a documented vendor format.** `[OFFICIAL]` **15 of the 36**
   are *paragraph* + `., ` + *comma-separated style tail* (`., halftone texture`,
   `., ukiyo-e style, Japanese woodblock print`, `., impressionist painting, visible brushstrokes`, …),
   and all 15 are the first 15 entries — a coherent family, not noise. It matches the technical report's
   caption pipeline exactly. Teach it as an available shape.

6. **`Rendered text goes in quotes` is sourced but undemonstrated.** `[OFFICIAL]` scoped absence:
   **0/36** prompts contain a double quote and **0/36** request rendered text. The rule stands on
   `docs/prompting.md`'s four sentences alone; the vendor has published **no** prompt+image pair showing
   Krea 2 rendering a glyph. Do not imply the technique is demonstrated.

7. **A Krea 2 CFG conflict is now dated to release day, not to a later card edit.** `[OFFICIAL]` The
   pinned release-day card (`665ef38`, *"Last updated: June 22, 2026"*) already reads
   `guidance_scale=3.5`, while `krea-character-art.md` §1.5 quotes the card as `guidance_scale=0.0`.
   The 09-03 digest's *Contradicts* #17 framed this as a newly noticed conflict; it is a **transcription
   error in our own corpus**, and the underlying question (does `Krea2Pipeline` treat `guidance_scale`
   as embedded guidance rather than CFG?) is still **open and testable**.

8. **The corpus's Krea 2 validator rule for `_cfg_pp` samplers has a substring hole.** `[SYNTHESIS]` on
   an `[OFFICIAL]` fact: ComfyUI v0.35.0's new CFG++ sampler is named **`cfgpp_ud10_ab`**, which does
   **not** contain `cfg_pp`. See *Tested findings* 1.

9. **`sdxl.md`'s Juggernaut family has an unrecorded licence split, and the app recommends the most
   restricted member.** `[OFFICIAL]` HF card YAML, accessed 2026-09-10: **`Juggernaut-XI-v11` and
   `Juggernaut-XI-Lightning` are `cc-by-nc-nd-4.0`** — non-commercial **and No-Derivatives, i.e. no

## Method & spend

**Access date 2026-09-10 throughout.** **33 fetches total** — 32 for Job 1 (the weekly sweep) and 1 for
Job 2 (the Krea harvest, which needed exactly one: the pinned raw README carries all 36 pairs). Both
under the briefed budgets (~50 / ~15). Plus local reads of
`docs/RESEARCH-PLAN-2026-09.md`, `research/digests/2026-09-03-digest.md`,
`research/_addenda/staff-claims-2026-09.md` (row 12) and `research/_addenda/krea-character-art.md`, and a
read-only grep of `PromptStudio.html` for `krea2:`.

**What worked.** `docs.comfy.org/changelog` (clean markdown, fresh, dated per release) and **PyPI project
pages** (upload dates per version) were the two reliable in-window detectors. The **HF models API with
`search=` and `sort=lastModified`** gave hard UTC timestamps for every model family in one cheap call
each and is the right primary tool for a weekly "did anything move?" sweep — ten model families were
cleared in ten fetches. `help.aliyun.com` exposes `meta-last-modified` and is therefore self-dating.
HF `/activity/community` pages fetch fine but their relative dates are unreliable (see *Errata* 4).

**What failed.** GitHub HTML and `raw.githubusercontent.com` were both stale (see *Nothing-found
register*), which cost this sweep every GitHub-hosted guide diff the brief asked for. Two fetches were
spent discovering it and one confirming it against PyPI — cheap, and the finding is worth more than the
diffs would have been. Two fetches returned oversized bodies and were read from the saved file instead of
re-fetched.

**Analysis method for Job 2.** The 36 prompts were written **once**, verbatim, into
`_addenda/krea-character-art.md`, and every statistic in that section was then computed by a
deterministic Python pass that parses the fenced blocks out of that same file. So the numbers and the
quoted text cannot drift apart, and the maintainer can re-run the analysis against the committed file.
Word counts split on ASCII whitespace; sentence counts split on `[.!?]` followed by whitespace and a
capital; category fill rates use fixed regex vocabularies which are reproduced in full in the script's
logic description in that section. Conditioning-position estimates use the two ratios already in the
corpus (5.6 chars/position from ComfyUI issue #14782's instrumentation; 0.75 words/token from `QJerry`)
and are labelled as estimates.

**Honest limitation.** No image in the Krea gallery was viewed, and no generation was run. Job 2 is a
**text-corpus analysis of an official prompt set**, not a test of what those prompts produce.
