## Contradicts our current guidance

1. **"Write prose because the encoder is an LLM."** arXiv:2606.03715, MIT/Technion, 2026-06-02
   `[OFFICIAL]`. A **"bag of position-tagged words"** — identity + order only, no context — matches
   full embeddings on quality *and* on attribute binding, spatial relations and numeracy; names
   FLUX.2's Qwen encoder as the assumption it questions. **Splits our doctrine:** "front-load the
   subject / order carries emphasis" is *strengthened* (four independent vendor sources converge —
   InstaSD/Krea 2, fal/klein, deAPI/klein, Civitai/Qwen-2512); "prose grammar is what makes it work"
   is *undercut*. Caveat: image models, not video. Also supplies a mechanism for our expansion split —
   rewriting that preserves salient words *and their order* should be safe; reordering should hurt.
2. **"Qwen-Image wants natural-language description."** Civitai 30826 (2026-05-31) `[LORE]` argues
   2512 was trained on **structured label data** and prefers `Subject:/Pose:/Camera:/Lighting:` blocks.
   Evidence weak (cites seeds and n=20 but shows no grids; tidy percentages; likely part-LLM-written)
   — **but directionally consistent with (1)**: a label list ≈ a bag of position-tagged words. Both kept.
3. **"No negative prompt on FLUX.2" — true, and hosts are mis-teaching it.** BFL `[OFFICIAL]`:
   *"FLUX.2 does not support negative prompts."* `diffusers` #13416 (open, unanswered since
   2026-04-04) `[OFFICIAL]`: `Flux2KleinPipeline` **and `ZImagePipeline`** expose
   `negative_prompt_embeds` with **no string→negative path**. Yet `[LORE]` **fal.ai actively recommends
   `negative_prompt`** and Segmind exposes it. **This extends the problem to Z-Image Turbo, which
   item C1 didn't capture** — the same bug as LTX-inert-negatives, wearing a third hat. Treat fal
   `/learn` pages as SEO, not documentation.
4. **`(word:1.2)` is CLIP-era and is now a mis-teaching on half our models.** `[LORE]` InstaSD: on
   Qwen3-VL, scaling one token "shoves the entire conditioning around… push past 1.2 and the picture
   falls apart." Real on SDXL fine-tunes only. `{}` re-confirmed **NovelAI**, colliding with ComfyUI
   wildcard `{a|b}`. New collision: **`(...)` is stage-direction action markup in Wan-Streamer**
   `[OFFICIAL]`, arXiv 2607.15038. Parentheses
   now mean three things — **no portable weighting syntax exists**. Untested on Z-Image and Qwen-2512.
5. **Our H3 hardware and LoRA lines are too absolute.** Corpus: "62+62 GB", "zero LoRAs". MiniMax
   `[OFFICIAL]`: 12 GB VRAM via pruned int8 (~42 GB download) for 480p+audio, and AI-Toolkit support
   with character/style/acceleration LoRAs "已在 Hugging Face 流通". **Cross-agent resolution:** likely
   not a contradiction — 62+62 is bf16 unquantised, and our LoRA claim was narrowly about **Civitai**,
   whose absence is neatly explained by the licence finding above (US platform, US-excluded weights)
   `[SPECULATION]`. Both lines need their precision/platform qualifier. Agent B independently found H3
   `embedding:` syntax in ComfyUI, which may also soften "H3 is prompt-inert".
6. **Krea 2 "no length limit"** — corrected by the `[TESTED]` item above.
7. **Three LTX-2.5 tensions, all kept as conflicts.** (a) The official **T2V** snippet passes the 2.5
   spatial upscaler while the **I2V snippet on the same page still passes the 2.3 one** `[OFFICIAL]`;
   that this is a docs bug rather than a requirement is `[SPECULATION]`, not cross-checked against the
   English README. (b) VRAM: official "as little as
   12GB" vs third-party "24GB+ combined; Gemma 4 encoder alone 15.37GB" — the 12GB figure probably
   assumes GGUF/offload. (c) `ltx.io/llm-info` promotes the Prompt Enhancer as a headline feature and
   never mentions it was default-on until 08-20, so a user reading official docs will not learn their
   early tests were invalid.
