📖 Format cheat sheet
[Shot 1] opens with a style keyword and has NO timestamp. Later shots: [Shot N] At MM:SS.mmm, the camera cuts to… — cut only when the shot adds new information; distance/angle changes are camera motion.

Camera = motion type + amplitude + speed as a natural sentence. ONE camera authority per shot; omit amplitude/speed when medium/normal — extra camera words spend adherence.

Dialogue: identity + delivery outside, exact words inside: The woman (S1) says: <d>[English] I get off at the next station.</d> Speaker IDs stay stable. On-screen text goes in "double quotes" verbatim.

Beat budget (official): 5s → 3-4 beats · 10s → 5-7 · 15s → 6-9. One primary action per beat — the x-ray above counts your sentences per shot against this.

References: every attached asset gets ONE job and must be cited or it bleeds into the output; two references claiming the same job compete for it. Retention markers: fully_preserved · partially_preserved · attribute_transfer · weak_reference (visuals) / fully_copy · partially_copy · reference (audio).
